Master of Science in Mathematics

Description

The MSc Mathematics program provides students with a strong mathematical foundation and also offers an opportunity to specialise in diverse areas such as pure mathematics, applied mathematics, data sciences and theoretical computer science. The curriculum has 14 core courses and several electives which gives the students plenty of freedom to focus on the areas of their interest. Furthermore, the curriculum is choice based that allows to student to chart their own path through the curriculum. Selected students will also have the option to pursue project(s) in place of some of the electives, helping them to gain hands-on training in a specific area. This curriculum will help graduates of this masters program to pursue careers in both academia and industry.

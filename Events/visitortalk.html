Mathematics of Machine Learning

📅 Date: April 20, 2025 ⏰ Time: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM 📍 Venue: Lecture Hall A-204, IIT Palakkad

📝 Abstract

This workshop is designed for students and researchers interested in understanding the mathematics that underpins modern machine learning. It will cover linear algebra, probability, optimization, and more, with practical examples and discussions.

🎤 Speakers

Prof. R. Subramanian (IIT Madras), Dr. Neela Nataraj (IIT Bombay), and guest contributors from industry and academia.
